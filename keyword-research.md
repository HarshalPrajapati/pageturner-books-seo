## Strategy

Prioritize the local transactional phrase “bookstores near me” on the home/contact pages, then use specific genre terms on category pages. The broad term “bookstore” has high volume but a KD of 57, so it is included naturally in the title and description rather than repeated. Competitor-brand terms were documented as research evidence but are not targeted because they do not represent Pageturner Books.

## Strategy

Prioritize local transactional phrases (for example, “bookstore near me”) on the home/contact pages, then use specific genre terms on category pages. Choose terms with clear relevance and attainable KD before broad, highly competitive terms. Use the gap report to build a small content plan around relevant missed or untapped terms.
